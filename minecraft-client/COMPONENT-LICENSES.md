## Private, noncommercial pack decisions

- Entity Culling is excluded: its publisher explicitly describes Modrinth- and
  CurseForge-hosted packs but its terms were unclear for this private file.
- The pack does not include JAR binaries. It points to official Modrinth CDN
  files, and users download those files from their publishers.
- Xaero permits noncommercial packs with attribution and official links; those
  links are included in `CREDITS.txt` and in the resulting pack.
- Crash Assistant asks packs to use official downloads where practical; this
  pack links to its official Modrinth file. LambDynamicLights restricts binary
  redistribution; its binary is not included.
- Sodium is referenced via its official download under the PolyForm Shield
  terms. Jade is attributed and the pack is noncommercial.
- The 50 inherited override files, including Mod Menu Helper.zip, matched the
  Fabulously Optimized v13.6.0 source tag byte-for-byte. The BSD-3-Clause base
  license is included in the pack; mod files remain separate upstream downloads.
